---
title: Smoke Signal
image: /images/smoke-signal.jpg
glass: rocks
description: Mezcal and amaro under a cloche of rosemary smoke.
date: 2026-09-03
ingredients:
  - item: Espadín mezcal
    amount: 50 ml
  - item: Amaro Montenegro
    amount: 15 ml
  - item: Lime juice
    amount: 15 ml
  - item: Agave syrup (2:1)
    amount: 10 ml
  - item: Rosemary sprig
    amount: 1
  - item: Dried orange wheel
    amount: 1
steps:
  - Smoulder the rosemary and trap the smoke under an upturned glass for half a minute.
  - Shake the mezcal, amaro, lime and agave hard with cubed ice for ten seconds.
  - Strain into the smoked glass over fresh ice.
  - Garnish with the dried orange wheel and the spent rosemary.
---
The smoke is doing something specific here: mezcal already carries smoke from the
roasting pit, and rosemary sits just to one side of it, so the two read as one
long note rather than two competing ones. Don't let the rosemary catch flame —
it should smoulder.
