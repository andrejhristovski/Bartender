---
title: Salt Flats
image: /images/salt-flats.jpg
glass: highball
description: Tequila and grapefruit with a chilli-salt rim.
date: 2026-08-31
ingredients:
  - item: Blanco tequila
    amount: 50 ml
  - item: Pink grapefruit juice
    amount: 40 ml
  - item: Lime juice
    amount: 10 ml
  - item: Agave syrup (2:1)
    amount: 10 ml
  - item: Grapefruit soda
    amount: 60 ml
  - item: Chilli salt
    amount: for the rim
steps:
  - Wet half the rim with a lime wedge and roll it through chilli salt — half, so there's a way out.
  - Build the tequila, juices and agave in the glass and fill with cubed ice.
  - Top with grapefruit soda poured from height.
  - Stir once, from the bottom.
---
A paloma with the salt taken seriously. Rimming only half the glass lets whoever
is drinking it decide how much salt they want, which matters more than it
sounds — the salt is what makes the grapefruit taste sweet.
