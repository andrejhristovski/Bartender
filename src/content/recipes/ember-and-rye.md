---
title: Ember & Rye
image: /images/ember-and-rye.jpg
glass: rocks
description: Burnt orange, rye, a long slow stir.
date: 2026-09-05
ingredients:
  - item: Rye whiskey
    amount: 60 ml
  - item: Burnt orange oleo
    amount: 10 ml
  - item: Demerara syrup
    amount: 5 ml
  - item: Angostura bitters
    amount: 2 dashes
  - item: Orange peel
    amount: 1 wide strip
steps:
  - Char a strip of orange peel over a flame until it blisters.
  - Combine spirits, oleo, syrup and bitters over ice; stir 40 turns.
  - Strain over one large block in a rocks glass.
  - Express the charred peel over the surface and drop it in.
---
A rye old fashioned taken darker: the orange is burnt over coals before it goes
in, so the drink reads smoky before it reads sweet. Stirred long over one block
and left to open up in the glass.
