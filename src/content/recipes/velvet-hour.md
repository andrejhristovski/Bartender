---
title: Velvet Hour
image: /images/velvet-hour.jpg
glass: coupe
description: A bourbon sour softened with amaro, served with a thick head.
date: 2026-09-01
ingredients:
  - item: Bourbon
    amount: 50 ml
  - item: Amaro Nonino
    amount: 15 ml
  - item: Lemon juice
    amount: 22 ml
  - item: Demerara syrup (2:1)
    amount: 10 ml
  - item: Egg white
    amount: 1
steps:
  - Dry shake everything without ice for fifteen seconds to build the foam.
  - Add cubed ice and shake hard for another twelve.
  - Double strain into a chilled coupe and let the head settle before serving.
  - Three drops of bitters on the foam, dragged through with a pick.
---
The last drink of the night. Amaro takes the edge off the lemon and gives the
finish something bitter to land on, which keeps a sour this rich from cloying.
The dry shake is not optional — it's the whole texture.
