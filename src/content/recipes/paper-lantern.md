---
title: Paper Lantern
image: /images/paper-lantern.jpg
glass: martini
description: Sake, yuzu and a green chartreuse whisper.
date: 2026-09-02
ingredients:
  - item: Junmai sake
    amount: 45 ml
  - item: Yellow Chartreuse
    amount: 10 ml
  - item: Yuzu juice
    amount: 15 ml
  - item: Elderflower cordial
    amount: 10 ml
  - item: Mint
    amount: 1 sprig
steps:
  - Chill the glass in the freezer for ten minutes.
  - Shake the sake, Chartreuse, yuzu and cordial briefly with ice — eight seconds, no more.
  - Fine strain into the frozen glass.
  - Slap a sprig of mint between your palms and rest it on the surface.
---
Light and dry, and easy to overwork. Junmai sake carries yuzu without letting it
turn sharp, but it bruises if you shake it like a sour — keep it short and keep
it cold. The mint is aromatic only; it never goes in the drink.
