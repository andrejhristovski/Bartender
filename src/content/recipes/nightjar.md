---
title: Nightjar
image: /images/nightjar.jpg
glass: rocks
description: Rye, sweet vermouth and a spoonful of cherry brine.
date: 2026-09-04
ingredients:
  - item: Rye whiskey
    amount: 60 ml
  - item: Sweet vermouth
    amount: 25 ml
  - item: Brine from the cherry jar
    amount: 5 ml
  - item: Angostura bitters
    amount: 2 dashes
  - item: Brandied cherries
    amount: 2
steps:
  - Stir the rye, vermouth, brine and bitters over cracked ice for 25 seconds.
  - Strain over one large cube in a chilled rocks glass.
  - Skewer two cherries and lay them across the rim.
---
A Manhattan with the sweetness pulled back and the cherry pushed forward — the
brine does the work sugar usually does, so the drink finishes dry instead of
sticky. Best made with a vermouth that has been open less than a week.
